India Air Quality Analytics

Data Analytics Project — Satyam Pratap

This project analyzes AQI records for Bangalore, Chennai, Delhi, Hyderabad and Mumbai.

Key Results

  • 12,042 combined records
  • Average AQI: 107.58
  • Highest city average: Delhi (208.29)
  • Lowest city average: Bangalore (74.32)
